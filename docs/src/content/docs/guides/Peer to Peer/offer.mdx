An **Offer** is a session type initiated by a client to establish a peer-to-peer (P2P) connection with an **Answer** client. It serves as the foundation for creating secure P2P interactions between clients.


### Who is this for?


- **Wallets/Credential Managers** looking to connect and peer with decentralized app (dApp) clients.
- **Fintech Applications** seeking to leverage decentralized peer-to-peer exchanges for financial transactions or identity management.
