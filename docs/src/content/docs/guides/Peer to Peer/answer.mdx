An **Answer** is a type of peer-to-peer (P2P) session initiated by a client in response to an **Offer** from another client. The Answer client plays a crucial role in establishing a P2P connection by presenting the [Deep Link](/guides/linking/#deep-link) to the Offer client and awaiting the response.

### Who is this for?


- **Decentralized Apps (dApps)/Wallets** looking to establish P2P connections with other wallets within the ecosystem.
- **Wallet Providers** seeking deep integration with `Liquid Auth` to offer a more tailored, secure user experience.
- **Blockchain Networks** aiming to facilitate secure, direct communication between participants.
